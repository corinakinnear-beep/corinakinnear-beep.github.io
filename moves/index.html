Bio

Read →

Choreographer, director and dancer. Founder of Parlā. Parlā is her company and the name under which she directs, choreographs and teaches, between Los Angeles, Berlin and Paris. She also designs spaces — the same attention to how a body moves through a room, applied to the room itself.
