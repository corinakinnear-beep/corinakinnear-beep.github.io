Nell'ombra

a dance opera in a venetian palazzo.

2017 Dance opera Palazzo Pisani Moretta, Venice January 2017

A four-hour dance opera for a gala evening in Palazzo Pisani Moretta, on the Grand Canal in Venice. The performers were the people of the walls — the palazzo's own residents come to life — appearing and disappearing in candlelight, shadow and reflection, so that no guest could be sure who was performing and who might be a mannequin.

In the candlelit entrance hall the characters stood as still as statues, then turned, or asked a passing guest for a glass of wine. Upstairs, eight rooms held eight different stories, so each guest saw a different show. A soprano and a castrato threaded arias through the night, and the whole evening drew on Venice itself: water, light and shadow, masks and mirrors, commedia dell'arte and Baroque opera — what is real, and what is not.

Each dancer built a character with Corina in the weeks before, and Tereza Kopecká designed their costumes as a contemporary spin on the Baroque — a theatrical recreation, not a reproduction. On a second evening the same characters hosted a masked ball to a Venetian quartet in period costume, handing out masks and teaching Baroque dances until every dancer had a guest for a partner.

It seems they were very convinced we were indeed people of the walls. Many asked which opera we were performing.Corina Kinnear, after the evening

The characters

From Corina's notes to the company, January 2017 — a starting point, not a script.

Miguel

Miguel Ángel Collado Sánchez

Living a life of contradiction and wandering without fear of consequence. No connection to the future, just the now. Inspired by the feeling of Don Juan.

Yuri

Yuri Shimaoka

Magical, mischievous and adventurous, with a purity and innocence. Inspired by Amélie, Juliet, Chloé in Mood Indigo and Laura in Paterson.

Wanderson

Wanderson Wanderley

Playful and testing boundaries, but also light-hearted. Pretending to get drunk as the night goes on, a reminder of joy and ease.

Cora

Coraline Arnaud

Sophisticated and almost arrogant, but it is a cover for an obsessive personality. She carries a deck of Italian cards, and the tasks surrounding them are enormously meaningful to her.

Laura

Laura Karlin

Formal and elegant, yet incredibly sensual and attracted to sensation. She wants a partner, and dances with the guests.

Rachael

Rachael Elliott

Comic and immature in a playful spirit, but in moments lost in dark nostalgia. Inspired by the films of Tim Burton.

Susanne

Susanne Eder

Feminine, with odd interests and obsessions. Unable to communicate with words — only with a kind of sign language, or pantomime.

Ryan

Ryan Walker Page

A castrato opera singer, with explosive outbursts of emotion and the mannerisms of a diva.

Sarah

Sarah Toth

An actual opera singer: moments of bravado with enormous character, and private backstage moments that are very honest.
