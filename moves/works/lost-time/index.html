Lost Time

2023 Dance film The Odyssey Theatre, Los Angeles July 2023

A short dance film made in Los Angeles in the summer of 2023, shown at The Odyssey Theatre.

Missing! Desperate to find lost time. Afraid it will never be found again. Lost yesterday or tomorrow, but could've been today. Changes depending on who is looking at it and where it's being looked at. Stands still for no man and flies when you're having fun. Loves to bother white rabbits. Priceless. If found, please contact right away. Not a moment to spare.From the film's notice
Choreography and direction
Corina Kinnear
Shown at
The Odyssey Theatre, Los Angeles — July 2023
