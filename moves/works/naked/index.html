NAKED: An Art Happening

2018 Production — 17 performances Odyssey Dance Festival · Pico Union Project, Los Angeles February–March 2018

How are we supposed to look? NAKED premiered at the Odyssey Dance Festival on 8 and 9 February 2018 and ran for fifteen further evenings at the Pico Union Project in downtown Los Angeles. At its centre is a thirty-minute contemporary dance for five performers of differing sexes, races and ages — from 23 to 73 — performed entirely in the nude, as a way of looking at non-sexualised nakedness.

Each evening was built as a happening in four parts: an exhibition of work by guest artists at eight, the dance at half past, then an experiment in which the audience was asked about its own perceptions and experience of nakedness, and finally a stripped-down, unplugged set by a different Los Angeles band.

Guest artists included the visual artist Gina Teichert, the illustrator C.W. Moss, the photographer Lilly Lawrence, the sound artists SILENTSHOUT and, in the photography exhibit Second Mirror, Andrew Grace. The bands included Whispertown, The Mynabirds, The Love Language, Russian Red and BLONDFIRE.
