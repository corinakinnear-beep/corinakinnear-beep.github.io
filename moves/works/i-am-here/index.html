I am here

2020 Commission Commissioned work, Amsterdam January 2020

A commissioned piece for an event in Amsterdam, January 2020: five scenes for a cast of dancers that move from a figure of depression — four costumed bodies stacked on one another's shoulders — through community and support to a single person walking, in slow motion, towards the light at the end of the catwalk.
