Before you begin

Instagram, once a day.

Briefly turns your feed from something you scroll into something you read — one organized brief, delivered on your schedule, written by Claude.

Please read before you continue. Briefly is not permitted by Instagram. Reading Instagram this way goes against Instagram's terms of use and will get your Instagram account suspended. Use it only if you accept that risk.

Sorted, not scrolled

Everything is filed into the categories you actually care about, instead of one long, chronological feed.

Written, not reposted

Your brief summarizes what happened. No re-posted photos or endless captions, just what's worth knowing.

Finite, not endless

You read it, you're caught up, you're done — no infinite scroll designed to keep you inside the app.

How it reaches you

Briefly isn't an app you install — it's a short, guided setup that turns Claude into your personal wire service for Instagram. Claude does the reading, sorting, and writing; you just tell it what matters and how often you want to hear from it. The next page walks you through both pieces.

Important: this is not permitted by Instagram. Automated reading of your feed goes against Instagram's terms of use, and your account will get suspended for it. Briefly is a personal experiment, not an Instagram-approved service.

What does delivery time mean?

Your brief starts building around the time you set — it may take a few minutes to actually land in your inbox.

What if Instagram blocks a run?

Occasionally Instagram may ask an automated-looking session to verify it's really you. When that happens, Briefly won't guess or send a broken brief — it'll tell you plainly that a run needs your attention. This is a little more likely with daily than with every-few-days or weekly.
