About

I'm Corina Kinnear, a designer with a background in dance.

I design spaces and interiors, apps, and movement. Years as a dancer and choreographer taught me to notice how a body moves through a room, where the eye comes to rest, and how a sequence feels from beginning to end. That attention to detail, to space and to flow is what I bring to every project — a floor plan, a screen or a stage.

Parla is where all of it meets. The dance direction lives at @parlamoves; the pages on this site are the tools and studies I make along the way.
