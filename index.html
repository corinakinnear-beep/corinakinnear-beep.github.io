About

I'm Corina Kinnear — a dancer and choreographer in Los Angeles, and a designer of spaces and screens.

I trained with Alonzo King LINES Ballet and have performed and made work in Los Angeles, Berlin, New York, Montreal and San Francisco, with companies including Invertigo Dance Theatre and the Deutsche Oper, and as a choreographer and creative director for events, brands and film. Today the dance direction continues under Parla — you can follow it at @parlamoves.

Design, for me, is one practice in many materials: a body moving through a room, the room itself, a screen in your hand. Alongside dance I work in space design and architecture and in app design. This site collects the pages and tools I make along the way — small, useful things, each at its own address.
