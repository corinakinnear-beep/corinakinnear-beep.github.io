Best times

    Search availability

    Ask in plain words and Ensemble searches everyone’s answers.

    Things it understands

    • How many and how long“two 2-hour sessions” · “90 minutes”
    • Or just the biggest overlap“as long as possible” · “as much time as we all overlap” · “the biggest block everyone shares”
    • Several at once“2 one-hour sessions and one 4-hour session”
    • Part of the day“3 evenings” · “two mornings” · “an afternoon”
    • Days of the week“not Mondays” · “Tuesdays and Thursdays only” · “weekends”
    • Clock times“after 6pm” · “before noon” · “between 10 and 2”
    • Who needs to be there“everyone” · “at least 4” · “must include Dana” · “without Sam”
    • A locationName it and it only searches there
