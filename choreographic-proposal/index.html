Choreographic Proposal

I’m interested in creating an ensemble piece with a lot of circular physicality, technical precision and detail.

I also anticipate theatrical interludes, weaving it together and creating a sense of humanity.

The proposal

A new ensemble work, shaped to the dancers and the rehearsal time available.

New work

Circular physicality

Ensemble phrase work built on rounded, circling movement that travels through the whole group.

Precision and detail

Technically exact material, set closely on the dancers in the room.

Theatrical interludes

Short theatrical scenes that weave the dance together and bring a sense of humanity.

I am developing several concepts for this piece. Some are more contained and some are more ambitious.

I would like to settle on one once I know which dancers are available and how much rehearsal time there will be, so that the work fits the cast and the calendar.

Biography

Choreographer and director. Founder of Parlā.

Corina Kinnear

Corina Kinnear is a choreographer and director, and the founder of Parlā, the company under which she makes work between Los Angeles, Berlin and Paris. Her dances, films and happenings take the details of ordinary life — time, nakedness, a crowded room, the sea — and give an audience a way to look at them again.

Her Los Angeles choreographic debut, MOVMT (2014), was an immersive evening for ten dancers between the ages of 22 and 74, joined by theatrical interludes. NAKED: An Art Happening (2018) was built around a nude company of five and a nightly conversation with the audience. Ocean12 (2020) was a happening on the beach at Santa Monica, followed by the dance film Lost Time (2023). Forgotten Time, made in residency at the Centre national de la danse in Paris, was shown as a work in progress at the Odyssey Theatre in 2025, and Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear premiered at the Los Angeles Dance Festival in 2026. She has also made commissioned work for events in Berlin, Venice and Amsterdam.

She holds a BFA from Dominican University of California with Alonzo King’s LINES Ballet, and has danced with the Deutsche Oper Berlin, Los Angeles Opera, Invertigo Dance Theatre and Alonzo King LINES Ballet, with creative residencies at PARTS in Brussels, CND in Paris and TanzFabrik in Berlin.
