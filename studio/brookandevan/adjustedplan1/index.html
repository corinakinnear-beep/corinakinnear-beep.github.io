What changed

Kitchen
Door 02 becomes a five-panel glass wall, 12'-3½" × 6'-8". The south panel opens as a door. The tall storage beside it is removed, and the counter continues 5'-6" south along the west wall, in front of the glass. The island is 3'-6" deep, sits 4'-0" from the counters and has a cooktop set off-centre to the west and four stools, two on each long side. The sink is centred on the window, and a 32" refrigerator stands on the north wall west of it, 8" clear of the west counter. A dishwasher sits east of the sink, and high cabinets fill the north wall either side of the window. A floor-to-ceiling closet, 1'-6" deep, replaces the bench on the dining wall; its west 2'-0", beside the glass wall, is shoe cubbies under a table top.
Master bedroom
Windows 07 and 08 become one pair of French doors, 6'-0" × 6'-8". The two south windows become one high ribbon window, 10'-0" × 1'-6", with its sill at 5'-5", and a king bed sits under it, with a bedside table and a pendant light on each side. The closet on the north wall is 2'-0" deep and a foot longer.
Arch
A wall with an arched opening now sits between kitchen and living. The arch is 10'-0" wide and 7'-10" at its crown.
Ceilings
Exposed rafters in the living room and exposed joists over the kitchen and dining, 2'-0" apart. Two skylights over the living room, each 2'-0" × 4'-0", set between the rafters.
Shelving
The wall north of the arch is opened up as open shelving, 2'-3" wide, 1'-0" deep, up to the kitchen ceiling at 8'-4". A potted olive tree stands on the second shelf.
Stair
A storage box under the stair, 2'-6" wide, 3'-0" deep, 1'-6" high.
Outside
Two wide steps lead down to the garden from the kitchen glass wall and from the master bedroom French doors. The bedroom's top step is 4'-0" deep and runs south to 2'-0" from the corner of the house, as a small bedroom patio. An outdoor shower stands on the west wall about 3'-2" south of the glass, in the private garden. Dotted lines show the path of each French door leaf opening all the way round to lie flat against the house.
Landscape
From the Dex Studio landscape framework: patio with outdoor cooking, a table for eight and seating; plunge pool with sun shelf; private garden, tree and planting line. Added: a ring of tall shrubs south of the shower that takes in the tree, made of two overlapping spiral arms with a hidden way in on the north side; and the future deck around the front porch.
Balcony
A floating balcony off the loft, 3'-0" deep and 10'-10" long, from the north edge of the loft French doors south to the end of the dormer. It is carried by the loft floor joists running out through the wall.
North bath
Turned a quarter turn clockwise, moved north so its north wall lines up with the master bedroom's north wall, and set against the east wall of the bath strip, with its door on the north wall. A passage 3'-0" wide runs along its west side to the master bedroom door. Beyond the passage is a closet, 2'-3" × 4'-6", entered from the master bedroom. The bath is 4'-4" wide beside the passage and 5'-0" wide south of it. The toilet is on the west wall near the door with 30" of clear width, a pedestal sink is on the east wall and the tub runs across the south end, with open shelving in the recess between the tub and the wall jog.
South bath
Takes most of the strip between the baths, so it is 7'-8" × 7'-8". The shower is across the north end with the toilet in the north-west corner, and one sink is on the south wall under a new window, 3'-0" × 2'-6".
Living room
A couch facing the fireplace, with one chair under the east windows and one set cater-corner beside the open shelving, facing the couch.
Office
A window, 3'-0" × 3'-6", in the south wall under the ridge. A wall on the east side, where headroom drops to 5'-0", with a low opening, 4'-0" wide and 5'-0" high, at its north end to the attic.
Hall
The hallway and the washer and dryer are removed. The bedrooms and bath open onto the main rooms. The wall south of the front door moves south to line up with the east bedroom door wall, which takes out the east closet. A floor-to-ceiling closet, 2'-0" deep, runs along that wall from the east bedroom door; the 4'-0" beside the front door is shoe cubbies with coat hooks above.
